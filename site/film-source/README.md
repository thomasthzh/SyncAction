# Product film source — v4

The 32-second film is an original product illustration, not a live recording. Its fixed1440×810
Canvas surface at `/film.html?render=1` provides `window.renderFilm(seconds)` after
`window.filmReady`. Rendering never sends business commands or grants permissions. The preview is
silent; the published MP4 contains an original32-second instrumental score.

## Direction

Four people enter progressively: one at0, then a second at3.000, a third at8.500 and a fourth
at14.000 seconds. Each arrow tip crosses the canvas edge continuously, with the name appearing just
after it. Everyone remains on screen after entering; all four participate throughout the second
half.

The opening browser expands into a full-frame landscape at the first drum entry. A large paused
clock and timeline give playback a macro shot. A light shared-page composition switches to a close
annotation surface; the fourth entry pulls the landscape back into a lit floating browser plane
under giant typography. The film then alternates light/dark and individual/group compositions,
before a focused black brand ending. Shots have different lengths and impact beats rather than
twelve equal instruction cards. Transitions affect content only. All four pointer tracks remain in
one continuous screen coordinate space.

`film-motion.js` owns four global Hermite tracks. Short purposeful movements, unequal reading holds,
shallow curves and occasional target correction replace continuous floating. Ordinary knots preserve
velocity. Clicks and UI confirmations share a timeline; seek and pen progress are also shared with
the drawing. All names are drawn before all arrow bodies. `window.filmState` exposes stable IDs,
positions, visibility, targets, chapter and transition state for independent acceptance. No random
jitter, orbit, cursor rotation, chapter teleport or unimplemented remote click is added. These
authored paths are not measured human traces; numerical bounds do not prove human realism.

## Sources and boundaries

The original Duet icon uses two unframed curves around shared space. Full/optical-small/monochrome/
dark-background masters live in `design/brand`. No Apple logo, footage, font, UI asset or wallpaper
is bundled. Apple official[AirPods Pro](https://www.apple.com/airpods-pro/) imagery and
[App Icons guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons)
informed our interpretation of oversized focal subjects, close-ups, negative space and simple
recognizable marks; this is an independent design, not an Apple affiliation.

`assets/film-v4-landscape.jpg` is an original AI-generated photographic still created with the
built-in imagegen tool, then encoded to JPEG. Its camera crop is animated as illustrative video
content. It is not actual footage. Generation brief: wide cinematic alpine lake at sunrise, dark
basalt ridge across the foreground, distant blue-black peaks, pale silver sky, a thin warm horizon,
realistic rock texture and atmospheric depth; no people, buildings, text, UI, logo or copied
wallpaper. The interface and pointer artwork remain code-native. The32-second music is original
synthesis, with entries/impact at3/8.5/14/28s and a natural ending; no external songs, samples or
voice.

Each user plays their own content. The illustration does not promise screen/video streaming, DOM
coediting, universal site compatibility, remote control of extension UI, or connection automatically
meaning media follows. Viewing a group differs from joining. Invitation acceptance and request
approval have different actors. First use is a separate new-device example; left-side companions
remain on another authorized page while the new site's permission is revoked. Revocation stops
future collaboration and does not delete already shared messages or URLs.

The homepage lazily selects versionedv4 media, with native controls, captions, transcript,
downloads, keyboard and reduced-motion handling. Closing unloads and returns focus. V1/v2/v3 media
and old website symbol URLs remain available. Validation includes motion regressions, brand
regeneration, independent120Hz sampling, actual encoded frames, completeH264/AAC decode and
real-browser playback.
